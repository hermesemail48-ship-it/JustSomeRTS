If you'd like to manually install a localization mod, it's very straight forward. Find the mod you'd like to use online somewhere (Check out risetoruins.com, steam forum or just google Rise to Ruins localization mods). Once you have your mod pack, simply unzip it into a subfolder in the /localization/ folder. Directly inside the subfolder should be the configuration.properties and text.yml file, as well as others if the mod needs them. Once installed, simply turn on the game and switch language packs by clicking the localization button on the main meny or select profile page!

Alternatively, if you're using Steam, you can simply visit the game's Steam Workshop ( https://steamcommunity.com/app/328080/workshop/ ), find a pack you want, click subscribe and turn on the game! Simple as that.
